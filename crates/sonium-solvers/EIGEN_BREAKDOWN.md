## Reading the breakdown

- LOBPCG cost is dominated by operator applies at small block widths; the dense remainder (orthogonalization + Rayleigh-Ritz, O(n·w²)) grows with block width and dominates for many modes — that crossover is the reason to watch the `dense` column, not just the iteration count.
- At this size (N=3600, 40 Lanczos vectors) the shift-invert Lanczos phase (~1.6 s of triangular solves + reorthogonalization) dwarfs the one-time sparse factorization (~30 ms); the factor dominates only at larger N or with fewer vectors. Each shift still needs its own factorization, so adding shifts adds a full factor + full Lanczos run each time.
- A shift landing on (or nearly on) a discrete eigenvalue makes `A - σI` singular and the factorization is rejected (`FAIL`): at N=3600 the spectrum fills [0, 8] with mean spacing ~0.002, so near-hits are the norm and the factorizer decides. σ=4.0 fails this way here (an eigenvalue sits at ~3.993).
