## Reading the tradeoff

- Total time to solution is `setup + iters × (matvec + apply)`; a heavy setup (AMG, Schwarz) only pays off when it cuts iterations enough to cover itself.
- Compare `ilu0` vs `none` on `convdiff-2d`: the ILU(0) setup is milliseconds while the iteration cut is typically 5-10x.
- On the SPD Poisson cases AMG should win total time only above some size; below it the setup dominates and `jacobi`/`ilu0` win.
- `helmholtz-2d` and `zero-diag` exist to show the failure modes: rejected setups (`FAIL`) and exhausted budgets (`STALLED`) are the expected, honest cells — a preconditioner that cannot be built is information, not a bug.
- `jacobi` never reports `FAIL`, even on `zero-diag`: tiny diagonal entries fall back to 1.0 (identity on those rows) by construction, so the setup succeeds and the solve stalls instead. That fallback is deliberate — Jacobi stays total — but on a zero diagonal it buys nothing over `none`.
- `ilu0` shows the other failure shape on `zero-diag`: the setup accepts the matrix (default pivot tolerance) yet stops after 387 matvecs without converging, while pivoted `ilut`/`block-jacobi` refactor through row swaps and converge in 4 iterations. Pivoting is the whole story on that matrix.
